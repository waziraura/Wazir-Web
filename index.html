GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Useful information about Wazir Aura, its services, and its current development stage.

What is Wazir Aura?

Wazir Aura is a growing digital platform concept for creative services, web experiences, portfolio projects, and future collaboration opportunities.

Can I discuss a website or design project?

Yes. Use the contact section to prepare an email describing your project, goals, requirements, and preferred way to communicate.

Is the marketplace fully operational?

No. Marketplace accounts, project publishing, proposals, hiring, payments, and related dashboards require additional backend development and security implementation.

Does the contact form send messages directly?

The current front-end form prepares a message in your email application. It does not independently deliver or store messages on a server.

Does the website play music or sound?

No. The cinematic experience is designed to be silent and uses visual effects and motion only.

Will the website work on mobile devices?

The layout is designed to adapt to desktop, tablet, and mobile screens. It should still be checked on real devices and browsers before considering the site fully verified.
