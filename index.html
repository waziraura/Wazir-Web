NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

What is Wazir Aura?

Wazir Aura is a creator-focused platform planned to offer useful digital tools, resources and freelance service information.

Are the tools free?

The listed starter tools are intended to be free to use. Features that require third-party paid APIs may need limits or a separate pricing plan.

Can I earn money using this website?

The website can support freelance services, digital products and future affiliate opportunities. Earnings are not guaranteed and depend on customers, traffic, eligibility and actual work completed.

Can I receive money through Easypaisa or JazzCash?

Those methods are planned options. Real payments and withdrawals require an appropriate supported integration or a verified manual payment process, plus the necessary backend.

Do I need to create an account?

You can explore public information without an account. Account-only features will become available after authentication is connected.

How can I order a digital service?

Visit the services section and use the linked Fiverr profile to discuss a project and confirm the scope, price and delivery terms.

How can I contact support?

Use the social profiles linked on this website or send a message through the contact form once a backend or supported contact service is configured.

Does this website guarantee income?

No. A website cannot guarantee a specific income or timeline. We aim to provide useful features and clear ways to explore services and opportunities.
