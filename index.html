COMMON QUESTIONS

Things worth knowing.

Some quick answers to help you get started.

Who can work with Wazir Aura?

Individuals, creators, startups, and businesses can reach out about design, website, and digital presence projects.

How do I get a project quote?

Send a project inquiry with your goals and requirements. Pricing and delivery timelines can be discussed after the scope is understood.

Can I create an account or leave a public rating?

Account registration, verified user reviews, and rating submission are not active yet. They require a connected authentication system and secure database.

Can I buy digital products or a membership?

Digital products and premium memberships are planned features. They will be offered only after the products, payment process, and delivery system have been configured.

Is there an admin dashboard?

A private dashboard for managing inquiries, users, projects, reviews, and sales requires a separate backend and admin authentication. It is not enabled on this static website yet.

How can I contact the team?

Use the project inquiry form or email zuhaibwazir558@gmail.com directly.
